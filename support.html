Welcome to ParCard Scorecard! This app helps you keep track of players, scores, and mini golf rounds.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a round?

Open the app and follow the in-app options to set up your players and begin recording scores.

How do I view previous rounds?

Use the round history features in the app, if available, to review previously saved games.

Where is my game data stored?

Game data is stored locally on your device, according to the app's current configuration. Deleting the app may remove locally stored data.
